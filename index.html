Privacy Policy

Spin the Wheel — Random Decision Maker

Last updated: July 17, 2025

🔒 Short version: Spin the Wheel stores all your data locally on your device. We use Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used — no personal information is collected.

👤 Who We Are

Spin the Wheel is an Android application developed and maintained by an independent developer. This Privacy Policy explains how our app handles your information.

Developer: Bonan01
Contact: privacy@spinthewheel.app

📦 Data We Collect

We do not collect any personally identifiable information. Specifically, we never collect:

  • Your name, email address, or account information
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • The content of your wheels or spin results
  • Cookies or tracking technologies beyond what Firebase uses

📊 Analytics (Firebase)

We use Firebase Analytics (by Google) to understand how users interact with the app in aggregate. This helps us improve the experience for everyone.

We collect anonymous usage events such as:

  • When a wheel is spun (number of sectors, spin duration)
  • When a result is shared
  • When a theme is changed
  • When a new wheel is created or edited
  • App session information (screen views, session duration)

None of these events contain personal information — we only log counts and settings, never the text content of your wheels. Firebase Analytics data is processed by Google and subject to Google's Privacy Policy. You can opt out of Firebase Analytics collection at any time in your device settings: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization.

🐛 Crash Reporting (Firebase Crashlytics)

We use Firebase Crashlytics to automatically detect and report app crashes. Crash reports may include:

  • Device model, OS version, and app version
  • Stack trace of the crash (code location)
  • Time of crash

Crash reports do not include any content from your wheels or personal information. This data is used exclusively for fixing bugs and improving stability.

📱 Data Stored Locally

The app stores the following data only on your device, never on any server:

  • Your custom wheel configurations (names and colors of sectors)
  • Spin history (results of your previous spins)
  • App preferences (theme, sound settings, spin duration)

This data is stored using Android's standard local storage (Room database and DataStore) and is removed when you uninstall the app. You can clear it manually at any time through your device's App Settings → Clear Data.

🌐 Internet & Permissions

Spin the Wheel uses the following Android permissions:

  • INTERNET — required by Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to send anonymous usage and crash data.
  • VIBRATE — for haptic feedback when the wheel stops. No data is transmitted.

👶 Children's Privacy

Spin the Wheel is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction). Firebase Analytics is configured to not collect advertising identifiers.

🔄 Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Analytics — anonymous usage analytics by Google
  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash reporting by Google

There are no advertising SDKs, social login services, or in-app purchases in the current version.

✏️ Changes to This Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy, the new version will be published at this URL and the "Last updated" date at the top will be changed. We will not retroactively reduce your privacy rights.
