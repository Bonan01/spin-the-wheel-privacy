Privacy Policy

Spin the Wheel — Random Decision Maker

Last updated: July 15, 2025

🔒 Short version: Spin the Wheel does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

👤 Who We Are

Spin the Wheel is an Android application developed and maintained by an independent developer. This Privacy Policy explains how our app handles your information.

Developer: Bonan01
Contact: privacy@spinthewheel.app

📦 Data We Collect

We collect no personal data whatsoever. Specifically:

  • No name, email address, or account information
  • No location data
  • No device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • No usage analytics or crash reports sent to external servers
  • No cookies or tracking technologies

📱 Data Stored Locally

The app stores the following data only on your device, never on any server:

  • Your custom wheel configurations (names and colors of sectors)
  • Spin history (results of your previous spins)
  • App preferences (theme, sound settings, spin duration)

This data is stored using Android's standard local storage (Room database and DataStore) and is removed when you uninstall the app. You can also clear it manually at any time through your device's App Settings → Clear Data.

🌐 Internet & Permissions

Spin the Wheel uses the following Android permissions:

  • VIBRATE — for haptic feedback when the wheel stops. No data is transmitted.

The app does not require INTERNET permission and makes no network connections of any kind. All functionality is fully offline.

Note: On some devices, fonts may be downloaded from Google Fonts the first time the app opens if they are not already cached by the Android system. This is handled by the OS and is subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

👶 Children's Privacy

Spin the Wheel is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction). Since we collect no personal data at all, this policy applies equally to all users.

🔄 Third-Party Services

Spin the Wheel does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, social login, or data-sharing services. There are no in-app purchases in the current version.

✏️ Changes to This Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy, the new version will be published at this URL and the "Last updated" date at the top will be changed. We will not retroactively reduce your privacy rights.
