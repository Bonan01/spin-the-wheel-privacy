Privacy Policy

Spin the Wheel — Random Decision Maker

Last updated: October 3, 2026

🔒 Your wheels and spin history are stored on your device. You can choose to export wheel backups. Google services handle advertising and optional purchases. This policy explains what is stored, what those services process, and the choices available to you.

👤 Who We Are

Spin the Wheel is an Android decision-making app developed and maintained by Bonan01, an independent developer.

This policy applies to version 1.0.1 and subsequent versions with the practices described below. Advertising and purchase availability depend on the app version and distribution channel. Test builds use sample advertisements and do not accept real purchases.

For privacy questions, use our GitHub Issues contact page. You do not need an app account to use the wheel features.

📱 Information Stored on Your Device

  • Your wheels, option text, colors, probabilities, and elimination settings.
  • Your latest 50 spin results.
  • Preferences such as the theme, sound, vibration, and spin duration.
  • A locally verified purchase receipt, if you purchase the option to remove ads.

We do not upload wheel names, option text, or spin history to our own servers. The app does not automatically back up this information to the cloud. Wheel features work without an internet connection.

💾 Optional Wheel Backups

From version 1.0.2, you can choose My Wheels → Back up wheels to export your wheels to a JSON file through Android's file picker. The file contains wheel names, creation dates, option text, colors, probabilities, emojis, and elimination progress. It does not contain spin history, app preferences, or purchase receipts.

Backup files are readable text and are not encrypted by the app. Choose a location you trust. If you select a cloud storage provider, that provider receives and handles the file under its own privacy practices. The app does not automatically send backups to us or to a storage provider.

Importing reads the file you select and adds its wheels to the app's local storage as new copies. The app uses Android's file picker for access to selected files; it does not request broad access to your files.

📢 Advertising and Privacy Choices

The app includes Google AdMob banner advertising. When advertising is enabled, Google's Mobile Ads SDK processes data for ad delivery, measurement, diagnostics, and fraud prevention. Depending on your settings and the advertising features in use, this includes:

  • Your IP address, which can indicate an approximate location.
  • Advertising, app-set, and other applicable device or account identifiers.
  • Interactions with the app and advertisements, and technical performance information.

This data is transmitted to Google over encrypted connections. Google may process it in countries other than your own. We do not provide your wheel text or spin results to AdMob.

The Google Play version uses Google's consent tools to request advertising choices where required. When available, you can reopen them through Settings → Ad privacy choices. The choices offered depend on your region and the applicable requirements. Ads may be personalized, non-personalized, limited, or unavailable. The wheel features remain usable if no advertisement is loaded.

Android also provides advertising-identifier controls; the options depend on your device. Google's processing is explained in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps and sites.

🛒 Optional Purchases

In the Google Play version, an optional one-time purchase removes advertisements. Google Play handles payment. We do not receive your payment-card details.

The app receives purchase information such as product and purchase identifiers, purchase status, and a signed receipt. It verifies and stores the receipt locally to remember that ads have been removed, including while offline. It checks purchase status with Google Play when connected.

You can restore a completed purchase using the Google Play account that made it. Google retains its own payment and purchase records under its policies. Purchase availability and restoration require a connection to Google Play.

↗️ Sharing, Network Access, and Permissions

If you choose to share options or a result, the selected text is passed to Android's share menu. The app or recipient you select then handles that text under its own privacy practices.

The app uses the internet for advertising, consent messages, and Google Play purchase checks. Optional vibration provides feedback during use. It does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, or precise location.

🗑️ Retention and Deletion

Your wheels and settings stay in the app's local storage until you remove them or clear the app's storage. History is limited to the latest 50 results and can be cleared in the app. You can delete individual wheels from your saved wheels.

Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling removes its local data, including any stored purchase receipt. Without a backup file or a separate copy of your options, deleted wheels cannot be recovered by us. A completed purchase can be restored separately through Google Play.

Files you export to a location outside the app are separate copies. Deleting a wheel, clearing app storage, or uninstalling does not delete those backup files. Delete them separately from the location or storage provider you chose, including any copies you shared.

Deleting the app does not delete advertising or purchase records held by Google. For those records, retention, deletion, and privacy controls are governed by Google's policies and account settings.

Questions you post on GitHub remain part of the repository's public issue history unless edited, deleted, or removed through GitHub's controls. You can request the removal of material you posted by contacting the maintainer through the same issue. GitHub may retain its own records as described in its privacy statement.

🌐 This Website and Support Requests

This policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. The page does not include our own analytics scripts or advertising. GitHub handles visits to its hosting service under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

If you open an issue, the maintainer can see your GitHub username, public profile information, and the message or attachments you submit. We use that information to respond to your question and address the reported issue.

GitHub Issues are public and require a GitHub account to post. Do not include payment details, purchase receipts, passwords, or other private information in an issue.

✏️ Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the app or its services change. The latest policy will remain available at this address, with an updated date. The app's Google Play listing and in-app privacy link will point to the current policy.
